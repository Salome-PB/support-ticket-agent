# Subscription Policy

> Fictional policy for RAG testing.

## 1. Basic Plan
The Basic plan costs $10 per month and supports one user.

## 2. Pro Plan
The Pro plan costs $30 per month and supports up to five users.

## 3. Business Plan
The Business plan costs $80 per month and supports up to 20 users.

## 4. Automatic Renewal
Subscriptions automatically renew at the end of the billing period unless canceled.

## 5. Cancellation
Customers can cancel from **Settings > Billing > Subscription**.

## 6. Cancellation Timing
Cancellation prevents future renewal but normally does not end access immediately.

## 7. Upgrade
An account can be upgraded at any time.

## 8. Upgrade Access
Higher-tier features become available after the upgrade is processed.

## 9. Downgrade
A downgrade normally takes effect at the beginning of the next billing period.

## 10. Downgrade Data
Customers should verify that their data and usage fit within the new plan limits before downgrading.

## 11. Failed Payment
A failed recurring payment may trigger payment retries.

## 12. Billing Restriction
An account may be restricted when an outstanding subscription balance remains unpaid.

## 13. Billing Date
The subscription billing date is determined by the original subscription or applicable billing schedule.

## 14. Invoice
Customers can view available invoices from the billing section.

## 15. Payment Method
Customers can update supported payment methods from billing settings.

## 16. Plan Limits
Each plan has different user, feature, and usage limits.

## 17. Additional Users
Adding users beyond the included plan limit may require a plan upgrade.

## 18. Refund and Cancellation
Cancellation and refund requests are separate processes.

## 19. Trial Period
A trial, when offered, may have its own expiration and conversion rules.

## 20. Trial Conversion
If a trial automatically converts to a paid subscription, the conversion terms are presented before or during signup.

## 21. Promotional Pricing
Promotional pricing may apply only for the period specified in the promotion.

## 22. Price Changes
Customers should be notified when a recurring subscription price is changed according to the applicable terms.

## 23. Subscription Reactivation
A canceled subscription may be reactivated if the account and plan support reactivation.

## 24. Business Administration
Business-plan administrators may have additional controls for users and billing.
