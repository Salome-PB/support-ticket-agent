# Abusive Content Policy

> Fictional policy for RAG testing. This document describes platform moderation rules.

## 1. General Rule
Users must not use the service to create or distribute abusive content targeting other people.

## 2. Direct Threats
Content containing credible threats of physical harm is prohibited.

## 3. Harassment
Repeated targeted harassment intended to intimidate or degrade another person is prohibited.

## 4. Bullying
Targeted bullying, including repeated insults intended to cause distress, is prohibited.

## 5. Hate-Based Abuse
Content attacking people based on protected characteristics is prohibited.

## 6. Slurs
Targeted use of severe derogatory slurs against protected groups is prohibited.

## 7. Doxxing
Publishing private personal information to facilitate harassment or harm is prohibited.

## 8. Personal Information
Users must not intentionally expose another person's sensitive private information.

## 9. Sexual Harassment
Unwanted sexualized harassment directed at another person is prohibited.

## 10. Non-Consensual Sexual Content
Requests to create or distribute sexual content involving people without appropriate consent are prohibited.

## 11. Extortion
Content facilitating threats to expose private information in exchange for money or favors is prohibited.

## 12. Impersonation for Harm
Impersonation intended to deceive, harass, defraud, or harm another person is prohibited.

## 13. Coordinated Harassment
Organizing groups to repeatedly target an individual for abuse is prohibited.

## 14. Threatening Messages
Threatening messages may be removed and may result in account restrictions.

## 15. Encouraging Abuse
Encouraging others to harass or attack a person is prohibited.

## 16. Evasion
Attempts to evade moderation controls for the purpose of producing prohibited abusive content are not permitted.

## 17. Contextual Discussion
Academic, journalistic, or analytical discussion of abusive language may be allowed when it does not promote targeted abuse.

## 18. Quoting Abusive Content
A quotation may be acceptable when necessary for reporting, analysis, or moderation review.

## 19. Enforcement
Violations may result in content removal, warnings, temporary restrictions, or account termination depending on severity.

## 20. Appeals
Users may appeal an enforcement action through the designated support process.

## 21. Repeat Violations
Repeated violations may result in stronger enforcement.

## 22. Immediate Safety Concerns
Credible imminent threats may receive expedited review.

## 23. Moderation Review
Moderation decisions may consider intent, context, severity, and whether a target is identifiable.

## 24. Policy Updates
The platform may update this policy as safety requirements and abuse patterns evolve.
