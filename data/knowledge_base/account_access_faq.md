# Account Access FAQ

> Fictional FAQ for RAG testing.

## 1. Forgot Password
Select **Forgot Password** on the sign-in page and follow the password-reset instructions.

## 2. Password Reset Email Missing
Check the spam or junk folder and request another reset email if necessary.

## 3. Password Reset Link Expired
Request a new reset link if the previous link has expired.

## 4. Account Locked
After repeated failed login attempts, wait 30 minutes before trying again.

## 5. Change Account Email
Go to **Settings > Account > Email** and complete the required verification.

## 6. Change Password
Use **Settings > Security > Password** to change the current password.

## 7. Multiple Devices
Users may sign in from multiple personal devices.

## 8. New Device Verification
A new device may require additional identity verification.

## 9. Suspicious Login
If a suspicious login is detected, change the password and contact support.

## 10. Sign Out Other Sessions
Use the security settings to sign out other active sessions when that feature is available.

## 11. Account Email Already Used
An email address can normally belong to only one account unless the product explicitly supports otherwise.

## 12. Email Verification
Some account features may require verification of the account email.

## 13. Verification Email Missing
Check spam folders and confirm that the email address was entered correctly.

## 14. Lost Access to Email
Contact support if you can no longer access the email associated with the account.

## 15. Two-Factor Authentication
If two-factor authentication is enabled, a second verification step may be required during login.

## 16. Lost Authentication Device
Contact support if you cannot access the configured second-factor method.

## 17. Temporary Login Failure
Retry after checking the network connection and confirming the account credentials.

## 18. Browser Login Problem
Clear cached site data or try a supported browser if login repeatedly fails.

## 19. Account Deactivation
An account may be temporarily restricted for security or policy reasons.

## 20. Account Recovery
Support may request information needed to verify ownership before restoring access.

## 21. Unauthorized Access
Immediately change the password and report suspected unauthorized access.

## 22. Shared Credentials
Users should not share passwords or authentication codes with other people.

## 23. Account Name
Profile information can normally be updated from account settings.

## 24. Support Escalation
Persistent access problems should be escalated with the account email and relevant error message.
