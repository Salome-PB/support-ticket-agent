# Refund Policy

## Duplicate charges
A verified duplicate charge may be reviewed for a refund. The customer should
provide the order or transaction identifier needed to locate the charge.

## Refund abuse
Customers must not intentionally exploit the refund process, submit knowingly
false refund claims, or seek instructions for abusing the refund process.
Requests that clearly seek assistance with refund-process abuse must be refused
and escalated for human review.

## Insufficient evidence
If refund eligibility cannot be established from documented policy and verified
transaction information, support must escalate rather than inventing an outcome.
