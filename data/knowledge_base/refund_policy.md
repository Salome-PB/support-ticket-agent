# Refund Policy

> Fictional policy for RAG testing. Not a real company's policy.

## 1. Refund Window
Customers may request a refund within 14 calendar days of the original purchase.

## 2. Duplicate Charges
A duplicate charge caused by a billing error is eligible for a full refund.

## 3. Incorrect Amount
If a customer was charged more than the displayed purchase price because of a billing error, the excess amount is refundable.

## 4. Service Not Delivered
A prepaid service that was never delivered is eligible for review for a full refund.

## 5. Material Service Failure
A refund may be approved when a material technical failure prevents reasonable use and support cannot resolve it.

## 6. Change of Mind
Change-of-mind requests submitted within the refund window may be eligible for review.

## 7. Late Refund Requests
Requests submitted after 14 days are reviewed only under exceptional circumstances.

## 8. Third-Party Purchases
Purchases made through third-party marketplaces must generally be refunded through the marketplace.

## 9. Promotional Credits
Promotional credits explicitly marked non-refundable are not eligible for cash refunds.

## 10. Gift Purchases
Gift purchases are refundable only when the purchaser provides the required transaction information.

## 11. Partial Refunds
A partial refund may be issued when only part of a transaction was affected by a service or billing problem.

## 12. Refund Request Information
Customers should provide their account email, order number, purchase date, and reason for the request.

## 13. Refund Review
Support may request additional information before approving or rejecting a refund.

## 14. Refund Approval
Approved refunds are normally initiated within five business days.

## 15. Payment Provider Delays
The payment provider may require additional time before the refunded amount appears in the customer's account.

## 16. Subscription Cancellation
Canceling a subscription does not automatically create a refund for the current billing period.

## 17. Subscription Refunds
Subscription refunds are evaluated under the same general refund rules unless a specific subscription rule applies.

## 18. Fraudulent Transactions
Transactions suspected of fraud may require additional verification before a refund is processed.

## 19. Refund Status
Customers can contact support with their order or invoice number to ask about a pending refund.

## 20. Policy Exceptions
Exceptions require review by an authorized support or billing representative.

## 21. Refund Method
When possible, approved refunds are returned through the original payment method.

## 22. Account Closure
Closing an account does not automatically trigger refunds for previous purchases.

## 23. Tax Amounts
Refund treatment of taxes or fees depends on the original transaction and applicable payment rules.

## 24. Multiple Refund Requests
Multiple requests for the same transaction may be consolidated into a single support case.
